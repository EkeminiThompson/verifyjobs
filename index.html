VerifyJobs.org - Free Job Scam Detector and Employment Fraud Checker

What is VerifyJobs?

VerifyJobs.org is a free online tool that instantly detects fake and fraudulent job postings. The service analyzes job descriptions using a sophisticated rule-based engine that checks over 50 scam indicators to identify employment fraud, fake job offers, WhatsApp job scams, cryptocurrency recruitment scams, advance-fee fraud, and other common job-related scams.

How VerifyJobs Works

The VerifyJobs job scam detector accepts three input methods: paste job description text directly into the web form, upload a PDF or Microsoft Word document containing the job posting (up to 10 MB), or submit a URL to a job posting for analysis. The analysis engine returns a comprehensive assessment including a risk score from 0 to 100, a legitimacy score from 0 to 100, a verdict classification (scam, high risk, suspicious, caution, or legitimate), a detailed list of specific red flags detected, positive signals indicating legitimacy, a plain-English explanation of the findings, and recommended action steps.

Scam Indicators Detected

VerifyJobs detects the following job scam warning signs and fraud indicators: requests for upfront payment for training, equipment, starter kits, registration fees, or background checks; promises of unrealistically high salaries for low-skill work, simple tasks, or data entry positions; recruitment conducted exclusively through personal messaging apps like WhatsApp, Telegram, or direct messaging rather than professional channels; use of free email domains such as Gmail, Yahoo, Hotmail, or Outlook for official company correspondence; urgent language designed to prevent careful decision-making, including phrases like "limited slots", "apply within 24 hours", "immediate start", or "act now"; vague or generic job descriptions that lack specific details about responsibilities, qualifications, or company information; requests for sensitive personal documents such as BVN (Bank Verification Number), NIN (National Identification Number), passport copies, or social security numbers before any formal interview process; references to cryptocurrency payments, Bitcoin wallets, blockchain investments, or crypto trading as part of the job; multi-level marketing (MLM) or pyramid scheme structures disguised as employment opportunities; commission-based work requiring personal investment; job offers that arrive unsolicited via social media, text message, or WhatsApp; guarantees of employment without interviews or proper vetting; requests to purchase products, pay for courses, or buy inventory before starting; wire transfer or Western Union payment requirements; foreign company hiring with no local presence or verifiable address; inconsistent or suspicious company websites with poor grammar, stock photos, or recently registered domains.

Common Job Scam Types Detected

VerifyJobs identifies these prevalent employment fraud schemes: Advance-fee fraud where applicants must pay upfront for training materials, equipment, uniforms, background checks, or visa processing; Work-from-home scams promoting data entry, envelope stuffing, product assembly, or survey completion with unrealistic earning claims; Reshipping or package forwarding scams that use victims to receive and reship stolen goods; Check cashing or payment processing scams where victims deposit fraudulent checks and forward funds; Pyramid schemes and MLM recruitment disguised as legitimate employment opportunities; Cryptocurrency investment scams recruiting "account managers" or "trading assistants"; Fake government job postings requesting payment for applications or exam fees; Personal assistant scams targeting remote workers with check fraud schemes; Mystery shopper scams requiring upfront payment for certification or assignments; Commission-only sales positions requiring inventory purchases; Romance scams evolving into fake job offers; Identity theft schemes collecting personal information under the guise of hiring; Phishing attacks using fake job applications to harvest credentials; Foreign employment scams promising overseas work with visa fee requirements; Freelance platform scams requesting upfront fees or off-platform payments.

Risk Score Interpretation

Risk scores are calculated from 0 to 100 based on the number and severity of scam indicators detected. Score interpretation: 0-44 indicates lower risk with few or minor warning signs, though users should still exercise normal caution and verify company legitimacy independently; 45-69 indicates suspicious postings with several concerning indicators that warrant careful research before proceeding, including verification of company registration, checking online reviews, and confirming job details through official channels; 70-100 indicates high risk or confirmed scam with multiple serious red flags suggesting the posting is fraudulent and users should not proceed, not pay any money, not share personal financial information, and report the scam to relevant authorities.

Legitimacy Score Interpretation

Legitimacy scores range from 0 to 100 based on positive indicators of genuine employment opportunities. Higher scores indicate more signs of legitimacy such as professional email domains, detailed job descriptions, reasonable salary expectations, standard application processes, verifiable company information, and absence of red flags. Lower legitimacy scores do not necessarily confirm scams but indicate fewer positive signals and warrant additional verification.

What to Do if a Job is Flagged as a Scam

If VerifyJobs identifies a job posting as a scam or high risk: immediately cease all communication with the supposed employer; do not send any money, gift cards, cryptocurrency, or make any payments regardless of the reason given; do not share banking information, credit card details, BVN, NIN, social security numbers, passport copies, or other sensitive personal documents; screenshot all communications, emails, WhatsApp messages, and the job posting for evidence; report the scam to your country's consumer protection or fraud reporting agency such as FTC in the United States (reportfraud.ftc.gov), EFCC or NITDA in Nigeria (efcc.gov.ng), Action Fraud in the United Kingdom (actionfraud.police.uk), Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre in Canada (antifraudcentre-centreantifraude.ca), or ACCC Scamwatch in Australia (scamwatch.gov.au); report the scam to the platform where you found it such as LinkedIn, Indeed, Facebook, or the job board; warn others by posting about the scam on social media, review sites, or scam reporting forums; block the sender on all platforms including WhatsApp, email, and social media; if you already sent money, contact your bank or payment provider immediately to attempt recovery and report the fraud.

Technology and Methodology

VerifyJobs uses a rule-based detection engine rather than AI or machine learning to ensure consistent, explainable results. The engine version 1.3 analyzes text patterns, domain reputation, language indicators, structural elements, and known fraud patterns documented in law enforcement databases and consumer protection reports. The ruleset is regularly updated to incorporate new scam tactics and emerging fraud patterns. Analysis typically completes in 2-5 seconds depending on input length and complexity.

Limitations and Disclaimers

VerifyJobs is a detection tool, not a guarantee of job legitimacy or fraud. All job seekers should conduct independent research including verifying company registration through official business registries, searching for company reviews on Glassdoor, Indeed, or Google, checking the company website for professional design and contact information, confirming job details through official company channels not provided in the suspicious posting, researching the recruiter's profile on LinkedIn, and trusting personal instincts if something feels wrong. A low risk score does not guarantee a job is legitimate, and a high score does not constitute legal proof of fraud. The tool should be used as one component of due diligence, not the sole decision-making factor. VerifyJobs is not affiliated with any government agency, law enforcement organization, or job board platform.
