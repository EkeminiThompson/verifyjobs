How VerifyJobs Works

VerifyJobs uses a rule-based detection engine to analyze job postings for scam indicators. We check over 50 different patterns, cross-reference them with known fraud tactics, and generate a risk score in seconds. Here's exactly how it works — no black box, no mystery.

The Process

1

You Submit a Job Posting

Paste the text, upload a PDF or Word file, or enter a URL to a job listing. We accept any format — WhatsApp messages, email screenshots, LinkedIn posts, anything.

2

We Extract & Normalize the Data

Our system extracts the text, cleans up formatting, and identifies key fields: job title, company name, contact information, salary, requirements, and descriptions.

3

We Run 50+ Scam Checks

The engine scans for red flags like payment requests, free email domains, urgency tactics, vague descriptions, unrealistic salaries, and suspicious contact methods.

4

We Calculate Risk & Legitimacy Scores

Each flag is weighted based on severity. The risk score reflects how many scam indicators are present. The legitimacy score shows positive signals (verifiable company, professional language, etc.).

5

You Get Your Results

We return a verdict (scam / suspicious / legitimate), a detailed breakdown of red flags and positive signals, and recommended next steps — all in plain English.

Understanding Your Risk Score

The risk score is a number from 0 to 100. Higher scores indicate more scam indicators. Here's how to interpret it:

70–100

High Risk / Likely Scam

Multiple serious red flags detected. Do not proceed. Do not send money, share personal documents, or provide banking information.

45–69

Suspicious / Caution Advised

Several warning signs present. Research the company independently before proceeding. Verify their website, check company registration, and search for reviews from employees.

0–44

Lower Risk / Likely Legitimate

No major scam indicators found. The posting appears more credible, but always verify employer details independently before sharing personal information.

Important Note

A low risk score does NOT guarantee a job is legitimate. It means we found no obvious red flags. Always do your own research: verify the company website, check their registration with local business authorities, search for employee reviews on Glassdoor or similar sites, and never pay money upfront.

What We Check For

Our engine scans for these common scam indicators:

Upfront Payment Requests

Any mention of registration fees, starter kits, training materials, equipment deposits, or "processing charges" before you're hired.

Free Email Domains

Company contact using @gmail.com, @yahoo.com, @hotmail.com, or other free services instead of a professional company domain.

Unrealistic Salary Offers

Extremely high pay for simple tasks like data entry, clicking ads, or watching videos (e.g., ₦500,000/week for basic work).

WhatsApp-Only Communication

Job offers conducted exclusively through WhatsApp, Telegram, or personal messaging apps with no formal company website or email.

False Urgency Tactics

Phrases like "limited slots," "only 5 positions left," "apply within 24 hours," or "first come first served" designed to pressure quick decisions.

Vague Job Descriptions

No clear explanation of duties, requirements, or company background. Generic phrases like "earn money online" with no specifics.

ID Requests Before Interview

Asking for BVN, NIN, passport copies, or bank account details before any formal interview or hiring process.

Cryptocurrency References

Mentions of Bitcoin, crypto wallets, or blockchain "investment opportunities" as part of the job offer.

What We DON'T Do

  • We don't contact employers. We analyze text only. We never reach out to companies or verify postings with them directly.
  • We don't guarantee accuracy. Our tool is very good, but scammers evolve. Always use your judgment.
  • We don't store your submissions. After generating your result, we delete the data. We keep no record of what you check.
  • We don't replace due diligence. Even if a posting scores low risk, you should still research the company independently.

How to Use VerifyJobs Effectively

  1. Copy the entire job posting. The more text we have, the more accurate the analysis. Include company name, contact info, salary, and full description.
  2. Check the source field. If you received the job via WhatsApp, select "WhatsApp" in the source field for more accurate detection.
  3. Read the full breakdown. Don't just look at the score — read the red flags and positive signals. Understanding *why* a posting is risky is more useful than the number alone.
  4. Cross-check with other tools. Search the company name on Google, check their website, look for employee reviews on Glassdoor, and verify business registration if possible.
  5. Trust your gut. If something feels wrong, it probably is — even if the score is low.
