What Happens After You Report?

  • We analyze the scam. Our team reviews your submission and extracts new fraud patterns to add to our detection engine.
  • We update our database. If the scam uses tactics we haven't seen before, we build new detection rules to catch similar postings.
  • We protect future victims. Your report directly improves the accuracy of VerifyJobs for everyone who uses it after you.
  • We keep your information confidential. We never publish reporter names, emails, or personally identifying information.

Thank you for taking the time to report. Every submission helps us stay ahead of scammers and protect job seekers around the world.
