Privacy Policy

We built VerifyJobs to protect people — that ethos extends to your data. Here's exactly what we collect, what we don't, and what we never do.

Last updated: May 2025
We don't store your job text
No account or email required
Never sold to third parties

1. About This Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how VerifyJobs.org ("we", "our", or "the service") handles information when you use our free job scam detection tool. By using VerifyJobs, you agree to the practices described here.

We are committed to keeping this policy simple, clear, and honest — no legal jargon designed to obscure what we actually do.

2. Information You Submit for Analysis

When you use VerifyJobs to analyse a job posting, you may submit one of the following:

  • Job description text pasted into the tool
  • A PDF or Word document uploaded for analysis
  • A URL to a job posting page

This content is processed in memory to generate your result and is not written to permanent storage. We do not save, index, or retain the text of your job descriptions, uploaded files, or the contents of URLs you submit.

Analysis results (risk score, verdict, red flags list) may be stored in aggregate and anonymised form to help us improve detection accuracy. This data contains no content from your submission — only numerical scores and verdict labels.

3. Automatically Collected Information

Like virtually all web services, our servers automatically log basic request data when you visit VerifyJobs. This includes:

  • Your IP address (used only for rate limiting and abuse prevention)
  • Browser type and version
  • The page or endpoint you accessed
  • The date and time of your request

This data is held in standard server logs, is not linked to your submissions, and is not used to build profiles or track individuals across sessions.

4. Cookies and Tracking

VerifyJobs does not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels, or third-party analytics services that profile users. We do not use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or similar tools.

The site may set a minimal session cookie to maintain your current tab state (e.g. which input tab is active). This cookie expires when you close your browser and contains no personal information.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with advertisers, data brokers, or marketing companies — ever.

The only circumstances in which we might disclose information are:

  • When required by law, court order, or valid legal process
  • To prevent imminent harm or illegal activity
  • In the event of a service acquisition, in which case any acquirer would be bound by this policy

6. Security

All data transmitted between your browser and VerifyJobs is encrypted via HTTPS/TLS. Uploaded files are processed in memory and immediately discarded after analysis. We do not store uploaded files on disk.

No system is perfectly secure. We implement industry-standard security practices but cannot guarantee absolute security of data in transit or on our servers.

7. Your Rights

Because we do not collect personal information tied to your identity, most traditional data rights (access, correction, deletion) do not apply in the conventional sense — there is no profile to access or delete.

If you have a specific concern about data that may have been inadvertently retained, contact us at the address below and we will investigate promptly.

8. Children

VerifyJobs is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has submitted information through our service, please contact us.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of VerifyJobs after a policy change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

We will not make changes that retroactively allow us to do things with your data that this policy currently prohibits without notifying you prominently.
