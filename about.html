Why We Built This

In 2024, job scams reached epidemic levels. Fraudsters exploit unemployment anxiety, targeting people in Nigeria, Ghana, Kenya, India, the Philippines, and beyond — markets where economic pressure is high and verification tools are scarce.

Most scam detection services cost money, require registration, or only work in English-speaking Western markets. We believe everyone deserves protection — regardless of where they live, what language they speak, or whether they can afford to pay.

Our Mission

Make job scam detection free, instant, and accessible to every job seeker on Earth — no barriers, no gatekeeping, no data harvesting.

What We Detect

Our rule-based engine checks for over 50 scam indicators, including:

  • Requests for upfront payments (registration fees, training kits, equipment deposits)
  • Unrealistically high salaries for simple tasks (₦500k/week for data entry, etc.)
  • Recruitment via personal channels only (WhatsApp, Telegram, personal Gmail)
  • Use of free email domains for "official" company contact
  • Pressure tactics and false urgency ("limited slots," "apply within 24 hours")
  • Requests for BVN, NIN, passport, or bank details before any interview
  • Vague job descriptions with no verifiable company information
  • Cryptocurrency payment references or wallet addresses
  • Offers that arrived unsolicited (you never applied)

Important Disclaimer

VerifyJobs is a tool, not a guarantee. A low risk score does not mean a job is definitely legitimate — it means we found no major red flags. Always research employers, verify company websites independently, and never send money or share financial information before you are hired through a formal process.
