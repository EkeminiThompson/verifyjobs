Privacy Policy

We built VerifyJobs to protect people — that same commitment applies to how we handle your data.

Last updated: May 10, 2026
We don't store your job text
No account or email required
Never sold to third parties

1. About This Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how VerifyJobs.org collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our free job scam detection tool. We prioritize transparency and minimal data collection.

2. Information You Submit

When you analyze a job posting, you may submit:

  • Job description text
  • PDF or Word document
  • URL to a job posting

Important: All submitted content is processed in memory only and is not saved to any permanent storage. We do not store, log, or retain your job descriptions, files, or URLs.

3. Automatically Collected Information

We automatically log basic technical information for security and performance:

  • IP address (for rate limiting and abuse prevention)
  • Browser type and version
  • Pages visited and timestamps

This data is not used for profiling or advertising.

4. Cookies and Tracking

We use no advertising cookies, tracking pixels, or third-party analytics tools (no Google Analytics, no Facebook Pixel, etc.). Only a minimal session cookie is used to remember your active tab — it expires when you close the browser.

5. Data Sharing

We never sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

We may disclose information only when legally required or to protect the safety and integrity of our service.

6. Security

All connections are encrypted with HTTPS. Uploaded files are processed in memory and immediately discarded. We follow industry-standard security practices.

7. Your Rights

Since we do not collect or store identifiable personal data from job submissions, there is no personal profile to access or delete. Contact us if you have any concerns.

8. Children

Our service is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. The "Last updated" date will be revised. Continued use after changes means you accept the updated policy.
